# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-10

This Privacy Policy explains how **Comic Book Generator** ("the App") collects, uses, and stores information when you connect third-party services (including Pinterest) and use the App’s inspiration/reference features.

## Summary

- **What we access (Pinterest)**: With your permission, the App may access your Pinterest account via OAuth to **read your own boards and pins** (read-only).
- **What we store**: The App may store **pin URLs and downloaded images** in your private inspiration library (locally on your device and/or in Supabase storage), plus optional notes you add.
- **Why**: To help you **organize creative reference material** for character design and comic generation.

## Information we access

### Pinterest (OAuth, read-only)

If you choose to connect Pinterest, we may access:
- Your Pinterest account identity needed for authentication
- Your own boards and pins (read-only) to support importing inspiration you select

We do **not** request permission to publish, modify, or delete your Pinterest content unless you explicitly enable such features in the future.

## Information we store

Depending on how you use the App, we may store:
- **Pin metadata**: pin URL and basic descriptive fields (e.g., title/description if available)
- **Imported media**: downloaded images associated with pins you import
- **Your notes**: optional tags/notes you add in the App

Imported images and metadata are stored as part of your private library:
- **Locally** (on your device), and/or
- **In Supabase** (database and private storage) used by the App

## How we use information

We use the information above to:
- Import and organize your inspiration references
- Allow you to search, filter, and attach references to characters/projects in the App
- Use references as **style cues** when generating character sheets or comic panels

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share data with service providers only to operate the App, such as:
- **Pinterest** (for OAuth authentication and reading your boards/pins)
- **Supabase** (for authentication, database storage, and file storage)

## Data retention

We retain imported inspiration items until you delete them from the App or request deletion.
