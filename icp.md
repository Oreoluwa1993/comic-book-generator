- **Secondary segment:** PR and public affairs firms serving Swedish political/corporate clients [CRM: Westander, Gullers, Rud Pedersen, Kreab, Diplomatcom, Prime Group, New Republic, Paues & Åberg — sent but fewer visible inbound responses]
- **Tertiary (warm, not yet converted):** Swedish political party press and communications teams [CRM: Centerpartiet (Johan Kling replied + referral), Socialdemokraterna (Dan Lundqvist Dahlin replied), Moderaterna, Liberalerna — mix of sent/draft/responded]
- **Company size:** Small to mid — 5 to ~200 employees; editorial or PA teams of 2–20 [CRM: org profile of responding contacts]
- **Region:** Sweden — primarily Stockholm-based media and PA organisations; Gotland/Almedalen context drove institutional outreach [CRM: all .se domains]
- **Who we sell to (role):** Editors, editorial directors, senior journalists, founders of media outlets; account directors / MD-level at PR firms; press secretaries and comms leads at parties [CRM: Johannes Lindström (Resumé), Brit Stakston (Blankspot), Rolf van den Brink (Dagensopinion), Ida Knudsen (MPF), Amos Friedman (Moderaterna), Adriana Haxhimustafa (S)]
- **Others in the buying decision:** In media orgs, often the editor-in-chief or founder decides alone; in PR firms, the MD or client lead; in parties, party comms head or press secretary [CRM: Champike Munasinghe (co-founder) facilitated internal intros — suggests the champion is not always the final sign-off]

## 2. Where they are

- **Geographic concentration:** Stockholm (dominant), with Gotland activated specifically around Almedalen week (late June / early July) [CRM: domains and outreach timing]
- **Context:** The Swedish political and media calendar drives the buying window — Almedalen is the single biggest activation event [CRM: "Almedalen" appears in subject lines of the 8 outreach threads that generated the most responses]

## 3. What they're trying to do

- **Job to be done:** Track which political narratives and media frames are gaining or losing traction in real time — before they appear in mainstream coverage — so they can respond, publish, advise clients, or brief campaigns faster than competitors [CRM: hooks in tracker notes: "narrative early-warning layer," "frames gaining/losing traction," "story gaps," "quote mismatches + framing divergence," "issue ownership"]
- **Trigger that starts the buying:** A high-stakes political event or media moment is approaching and they realise their existing tools (manual monitoring, search, RSS) are too slow or too shallow [CRM: Almedalen 2026 was the trigger for all June outreach; Rolf van den Brink (Dagensopinion) had the most exchanges suggesting he was actively evaluating before the event]
- **Urgency signal:** Time-to-event — outreach that named a specific upcoming deadline (Almedalen, specific dates) generated faster responses and demo bookings than generic hooks [CRM: Blankspot demo booked 3 days after first contact; MPF demo 2 days after; Resumé 4 days after — all Almedalen-framed threads]

Stewart Stanbra (Pinecone Impact) was introduced and a meeting was confirmed. - **What they search for / how they find solutions:** (assumption — not visible in this CRM) Likely Nordic media intelligence tools, PR monitoring software; may also respond to Almedalen-week networking and editorial-circle word of mouth [CRM: no inbound discovery data; follow up with GA4 or first-meeting survey]
- **Optimal outreach window:** Immediately before a major political event (Almedalen, election, budget week) — urgency accelerates the cycle from cold to demo in under a week [CRM: all demo bookings happened within 4 days of first contact during Almedalen sprint]

## 6. Who this is NOT (disqualifiers)

- **Individual politicians (wrong entry point):** 6 individual politician emails were still in "Drafted - awaiting approval" at sprint end — gatekeeping is high, budget authority sits elsewhere, and outreach to personal political email addresses may not be appropriate. The right entry is the party's press or comms team, not the individual. [CRM: tracker rows 17–24 all "Drafted - awaiting approval"; contrast with party comms contacts that were sent and replied]
- **Gotland / one-event institutions:** Gotland municipality (Peter Häggbom Norrby, Petra Jonsson) engaged with demos but both closed — likely interested in the Almedalen context only, no recurring need or budget for a standing subscription. [CRM: tracker rows 40–41 "Responded - closed"]
- **One-off event curiosity without a monitoring mandate:** Contacts who reply but frame the interest as "before Almedalen" only — without an ongoing intelligence or client-reporting need — will not convert to paid. The paying customer has a permanent narrative-monitoring problem, not a one-time event question. [CRM: Gotland pattern; contrast with Blankspot/MPF/Resumé who have permanent media/journalism roles]
- **Generic PR firms without political/public affairs clients:** (assumption) PA-specialist firms (Westander, Rud Pedersen, Kreab) are logical fits; generalist creative agencies without policy or political exposure are not — Terminal's value is in political/media narrative tracking, not brand comms. [CRM: hook language targeted PA-specific language; assumption on agency type — verify in next round]
