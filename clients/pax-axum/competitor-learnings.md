## Standing rules (from pre-run research)

1. **Beneath Ceaseless Skies is the ideal short fiction market** for this author's work. It publishes "literary adventure fantasy" in secondary worlds and explicitly welcomes debut writers. African and ancient civilization-inspired world-building is underrepresented in their catalog — this is a first-mover opportunity. Submit a standalone short story from the world of The Wooden Bible.

2. **The agent pitch should not use "diverse" as a descriptor.** Every agent submission from underrepresented authors uses this word. It signals nothing. Instead: name the specific culture, the specific mythology, the specific world element.

3. **The trad pub vs serialization tension is the central strategic decision.** If Book 1 goes to Royal Road for free, it weakens the traditional publishing query. The recommended path: query Book 1 via agents, serialize a prequel or Book 2 on Royal Road to build audience. Do not do both with the same text.

4. **Inkitt and Galatea must be avoided entirely** given the comics/animation ambitions. Their contracts create rights complications that could block adaptation licensing deals.

5. **The "8-book series" framing is a competitive differentiator with agents.** Most debuts pitch a trilogy or standalone. A fully planned 8-book series signals commitment and IP depth — use it.
