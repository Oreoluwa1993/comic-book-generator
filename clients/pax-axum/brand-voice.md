## Tone

**Overall:** Serious, world-built, mythologically grounded. Not grimdark. Not humorous or light. Epic in scope, personal in execution.

**For agent copy:** Professional, specific, confident without arrogance. Every sentence earns its place.

**For reader copy:** Direct and evocative. The world does the selling — describe it, don't market it.

**For community copy (r/worldbuilding, Discord):** Curious, generous, willing to go deep on the lore. Don't ask for anything.

**For Patreon copy:** Insider language. Patrons are collaborators in the world, not customers. Speak as if letting them behind the curtain.

## Use these phrases

These are the copy angles that differentiate The Wooden Bible from the noise:

- "A fantasy world rooted in the Kingdom of Benin, the Mongol Empire, the Moorish empires of Spain, and the Aztec and Inca civilisations" — name every source, never "African fantasy" or "diverse"
- "The eighth book is as planned as the first" — signals series depth and IP value
- "The mythology came first" — establishes that this is world-built from the ground up, not plot-driven with a fantasy skin
- "Nine years in the making" — the encyclopaedia is a proof point for depth
- "Founding reader" — makes early readers feel like discoverers
- "Insider of the world" — Patreon framing; patrons get classified documents, not just advance chapters
- "Comics, animation — the world was built for every medium" — to agents and pipeline targets
- "Written across continents" — for interviews and community; the correspondence origin story is compelling

## Avoid these phrases

| Phrase | Why |
|---|---|
| "Diverse fantasy" | Invisible and generic — every publisher says this now |
| "Unlike anything you've read before" | Every ARC post says this — signals nothing |
| "Epic adventure" | Meaningless as a descriptor |
| "Chosen one," "prophecy," "ancient evil" | Only use if this is genuinely present AND it's a subversion |
| "Self-published" | Do not use in agent queries |
| "I've already released it online" | Do not use in agent queries |
| "Support my work" | Patreon framing — patrons are insiders, not donors |
| "I hope you enjoy" | Passive — cut it |

## Publishing strategy note

The authors plan to self-publish while simultaneously querying agents. This creates a real tension:

- If Vol 1 is **posted free anywhere** (Royal Road, Wattpad, etc.) before a publisher buys rights, most agents will decline — publishers cannot acquire first serial rights for content already freely distributed.
- If Vol 1 is **self-published as a paid ebook**, some agents will still query (especially if sales prove the market). Others will decline because they need first rights. This is the higher-risk version of the plan.
- If Vol 1 is **kept off all platforms** while querying, agents have full commercial rights to offer publishers — strongest position for a deal.

**Recommended path (protects all options):**
1. Query agents with Vol 1 while it is not yet published anywhere (6-12 month window)
2. Simultaneously: post a prequel short story or standalone excerpt on Royal Road to start building audience — NOT the Vol 1 text
3. Launch Patreon/Ream the same week as any Royal Road posting
4. If no agent offer after 12+ months, self-publish Vol 1 (paid ebook + print on demand) and shift to hybrid path

## Email / query letter structure

For agent queries:
1. **Hook** — name the protagonist + world + central conflict in one sentence
2. **Comp** — "For readers of [A] and [B]"
3. **World premise** — what makes this world specifically different (the cultural foundation, the mythology structure, the magic system origin)
4. **Series plan** — 8-book arc, where Book 1 fits, what it resolves vs sets up
5. **Multimedia note** — brief; only if it's genuine
6. **Bio** — writing credentials (competitions, magazine credits, workshop attendance), author identity if relevant to grants/grants-adjacent credibility
7. **CTA** — "I'd be glad to send the full manuscript at your request."

For ARC reader posts:
1. Cultural hook (first sentence — what civilization inspired this)
2. One sentence on what the world does differently
3. Series length (8+ books = commitment signal)
4. Ask (ARC in exchange for honest review)
5. Link

For Patreon posts:
1. What's exclusive this month (specific — name the lore drop)
2. Advance chapter count
3. World connection (why this patron content matters to the story)
4. CTA with tier name and price

## CTA rules

- Agent queries: "I'd be glad to send the full manuscript at your request." (Period. No hedging.)
- ARC asks: "Request your ARC at [link]. Launch date: [date]. Limited to [N] readers."
- Patreon: "Join at the [Advance Reader — $5/month] tier for 10 advance chapters starting today."
- Social/community: No CTA on lore posts. Let the world speak. Add a link in bio, not in the post.

Reading The Wooden Bible: that there is a world behind the book — a full civilization with mythology, history, politics, and cosmology that extends far beyond any single chapter.
