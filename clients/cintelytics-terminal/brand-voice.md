## Tone

Direct and confident. Not breathless. Not startup hype.
The voice of someone who sees the signal before anyone else — and says so plainly.
No AI buzzwords. No superlatives. No passive urgency.
Write in Swedish when addressing Swedish media or political contacts directly.

---

## Use these exact phrases

These are confirmed to land or map directly to unique product capabilities.
At least one must appear in every email and social post.

- "narratives gaining or losing traction"
- "narrative early-warning layer"
- "frames" / "framing divergence" / "framing analysis"
- "story gaps"
- "issue ownership"
- "pre-wave" — detecting stories before they cross from alternative media to mainstream
- "narrative velocity" — how fast a story is accelerating through the ecosystem
- "blind spot" — what institutions do that media doesn't cover
- "quote verification" — cross-referencing attributed statements against source records
- "source vs story gap" — the measurable gap between institutional record and media output
- "information gap" — structural, not a media failure
- "cross-domain" — parliament activity + media coverage + incident data simultaneously

## Never use these phrases

| Phrase | Why |
|---|---|
| "AI-powered" in subject lines | Signal AI owns this lane; mechanism framing, not outcome |
| "platform" as standalone noun | Table stakes language |
| "comprehensive coverage" or "all sources" | Meltwater/Retriever claim this |
| "stay informed" or "monitor everything" | Saturated, implies reactive not predictive |
| "Narrative ROI" | Investor framing — not customer language |
| "learn more" as CTA | Not a proof of outcome |
| "let's chat" as CTA | Passive, no urgency |
| "whenever you're ready" | Passive urgency |
| "let me know if interested" | Passive urgency |

## CTA rules

Every CTA must offer a specific proof of outcome — not a general ask.

**What works:**
- "See a live narrative velocity chart on a story from this week"
- "I can show you the quote verification running on [specific outlet]"
- "Book 20 minutes — I'll show you the blind spot Terminal caught before it hit [Swedish publication]"

**What doesn't work:**
- "Learn more"
- "Would love to connect"
- "Happy to jump on a call"

---

## Email structure (per persona)

Every email follows this sequence:

1. **Sentence 1:** Their current obstacle — name the *consequence* of their current tools failing (not the tool). No product mention.
2. **Sentences 2–3:** The trigger — name the specific event or date making this urgent now.
3. **Sentence 4+:** Introduce Terminal as the mechanism that enables the job. Keep brief. No feature list.
4. **Proof point:** One specific, concrete example of the outcome.
5. **CTA:** The specific proof you're offering in the demo.

## Persona-specific voice notes

**Persona A (Media / Editorial):** Lead with narrative velocity and pre-wave detection. Swedish-language subject or opener for Stockholm media contacts.

**Persona B (Political Party Comms):** Lead with coalition intelligence and the Riksdag-specific capability. Name the 2026 election cycle. Never address the MP directly — always the comms director.

**Persona C (PA / Corporate Comms):** Lead with the source-vs-story gap. "Respond to your client's question before they finish asking it."

**Persona D (Research / News Agency):** Lead with quote verification and systematically measurable information gaps.

**Persona E (B2G / AI Audit):** Lead with narrative injection risk and the 2026 election as the wedge. Different sales motion — do not blend with A–D copy.

## Proof points available in demo (for CTA specificity)

- Narrative velocity chart: real story, live acceleration data
- Quote verification: example of attributed statement vs. source record
- Blind spot report: what institutions did that media didn't cover (named example)
- Framing divergence: left/center/right outlet comparison on same event
- Pre-wave flag: story that was accelerating in alternative media before mainstream pickup
- Riksdag coalition matrix: bill survival and rebel-MP signals (for Persona B)
- Geographic reality gap: municipality-level under-reporting example (for Persona D)
